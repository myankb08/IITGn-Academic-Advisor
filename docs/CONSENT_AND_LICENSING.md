# Consent and licensing

This note covers every kind of data the project collects or produces for Part A and later parts.

## Institute documents

The files in `data/raw/sources/` are IIT Gandhinagar academic regulations (Academic Affairs Advisories 5, 8, 17, 24, 42, 60 and the Honour Code), the revised CSE M.Tech course structure for 2025-26, and the Sem-I 2026-27 timetable. They are public academic regulations and timetables published by the institute for its students. We use them only for non-commercial coursework (CS 203, Project 4). The files are kept untouched, their sha256 values are in `data/raw/sources/source_metadata.json`, and every derived record points back to the file and page or row it came from. We do not redistribute them outside the course submission.

## EE M.Tech site

hem436 supplied the URL of the EE department's public M.Tech site (https://sites.google.com/iitgn.ac.in/ee-mtech/). The pipeline saved the home page and the three specialisation pages as raw HTML in `data/raw/sources/ee_mtech_site/`, with sha256 and fetch time in `source_metadata.json` and `supporting_files/A_collection/ee_site_fetch_log.csv`. This is public institute content, used the same way as the advisories: non-commercial coursework, every derived rule or course points back to the page and section. The pages also list faculty names and research areas. Those sections are kept in the raw extraction but are not used in any processed file.

## Course plans

Course plans in `data/raw/course_plans/` were downloaded from the links in the "Link To Course Plan" column of the public timetable. Only links that open without signing in were saved. Links that need an IITGN login were not bypassed, they are listed as failed in `supporting_files/A_collection/course_plan_fetch_log.csv`. Plans are used only to read prerequisite statements. Instructor names inside plans are not copied into any processed file.

## Google Forms respondents

Part of the evaluation uses a Google Form filled by students. The rules for that data:

- Each respondent gets an anonymised ID (for example `R001`). We do not store names, email addresses, roll numbers or IP addresses. The form is set to not collect email addresses.
- The first question is a required consent checkbox (exact wording kept in `docs/GOOGLE_FORM.md`):
  "I agree that my anonymous answers may be used in a course project dataset". The help text says answers are stored without names or emails and may be published as part of the project dataset.
- Only respondents who tick the box are kept. Consent is logged in `data/consent_register.csv`, one row per response.
- No responses have been collected yet, so the register holds only its header.
- The Google timestamp is deleted before use, and email addresses or phone numbers typed into free text are removed by `src/collect/import_forms.py`.

## Synthetic students

Student transcripts in `data/processed/students.jsonl` are generated by the rule engine from a fixed seed in `params.yaml`. They are not based on any real student and contain no personal data.

## Model outputs

Paraphrases and answers produced by local open-weight models are stored with the model name and tag. They are generated text, not personal data.
