# Dataset card: IITGN M.Tech course advisory pairs

## Summary
Question and answer pairs for course advising in four IIT Gandhinagar M.Tech programmes (MTech-CSE, MTech-AI,
MTech-EE, MTech-ICDT). Each pair has a synthetic student with a transcript, a templated question, an LLM paraphrase
of the question, a gold answer computed by a rule engine (`gold_json`), a deterministic text justification
(`gold_text`) and an LLM rewrite of that justification. A human-audited subset of the test split is the gold test
set. Schemas: `docs/DATA_CONTRACT.md`. Counts below are for the original (non-augmented) pairs as of the build on
2026-10-17 (augmented children and paraphrases are being regenerated for the new pairs); the exact current numbers are always in `supporting_files/E_splits/split_summary.json`.

## Splits and balance (original pairs)
Split by student (no student in two splits), with one held-out surface template per family used only in test, and
MTech-ICDT as a separate programme-shift set. Students are split first and each pair is rendered with a template
allowed in its split, so no pair is dropped. See HANDOFF_A2.md for the rules.

| split | students | pairs | remaining_core | eligibility (eligible / not) | elective_recommendation | MTech-CSE | MTech-AI | MTech-EE | MTech-ICDT |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| train | 101 | 336 | 112 | 112 (56 / 56) | 112 | 127 | 90 | 119 | 0 |
| val | 24 | 80 | 27 | 26 (13 / 13) | 27 | 35 | 21 | 24 | 0 |
| test | 31 | 102 | 34 | 34 (17 / 17) | 34 | 49 | 21 | 32 | 0 |
| programme_holdout | 24 | 82 | 27 | 28 (14 / 14) | 27 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 82 |

In all 600 pairs, eligibility answers are balanced by design (100 eligible, 100 not; blocking reasons ALREADY_TAKEN 34,
NOT_OFFERED_IN_PARITY 37, NOT_OFFERED_NEXT_TERM 40). The test split has 102 pairs (34 per intent), and both labelling
rounds come from it. Per-programme test counts are uneven (MTech-AI 21), so report per-programme scores with counts.

## Known biases and limits
- **Synthetic students.** Transcripts are generated by the rule engine to be valid, so they are cleaner than real
  ones: no failed courses, no substitutions, no exceptions approved by a committee. Programme mix follows
  params.yaml (CSE 0.35, AI 0.2, EE 0.3, ICDT 0.15), not real intake.
- **No enforceable prerequisites in the sources.** 103 of 104 courses have `prereq_source: none_stated`, one has a
  manual entry. Course plans name background (often "or equivalent") that is stored as recommended background and
  only warns (D4). Questions about prerequisites therefore mostly test the offering and already-taken rules.
- **Credit defaults.** 51 courses have no credit value in the timetable or the documents and take a documented
  default (`credits_source` starts with `default:`). Credit-gap answers inherit that assumption.
- **ICDT list incomplete.** The EE site marks the ICDT discipline list "to be updated soon", so ICDT answers rest on
  a partial list. This is one more reason ICDT is evaluated only as a held-out programme.
- **Timetable for one term only.** Only Sem-I 2026-27 is known. Sem-II eligibility answers carry
  OFFERING_UNKNOWN unless a parity is stated.
- **Small real-query set.** The real Google Forms questions (shifted split) are few and come from students who
  chose to answer; they are for a distribution-shift check, not for training.
- **Small CPU paraphraser.** Paraphrases come from qwen2.5:1.5b (Q4_K_M) on a laptop CPU. It drops or changes course
  codes and constraints at a noticeable rate; outputs are kept as returned and the human audit measures this.
  Augmentation children are kept only if the rule engine gives the same (or a correctly recomputed) answer.
- **Templated questions.** 22 English templates in 7 families; real students phrase things differently, which is
  what the held-out templates, the paraphrases and the shifted split try to measure.

## Intended use
Training and evaluating a small advisory model for IITGN M.Tech students, and studying label noise and
augmentation invariance in that setting (CS203 Assignment 1 and 2). Not a source of official academic advice:
answers follow the documents as of the 2026-27 Sem-I timetable and the team's documented interpretations, and real
decisions must be checked with the academic office. No personal data: students are synthetic and the forms data is
anonymised and consent-filtered (`docs/CONSENT_AND_LICENSING.md`).
